# About Polyram
Polyram Group is a global provider of high-performance thermoplastic compounds and specialty polymer solutions. 

Founded in Israel, the company develops and manufactures customized materials used across industries such as automotive, electrical & electronics, construction, and consumer goods. 

Polyram focuses on innovation, offering advanced engineering plastics and sustainable material solutions that meet strict performance and regulatory requirements. 

With international production and distribution capabilities, the group serves customers worldwide, delivering tailored products and technical expertise.
